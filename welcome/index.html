Questions

Good to know.

Is it free?

Yes. Faithful Portion will always be free to use: record, track and manage your money well, at no cost. Paid features may be added later for those who want advanced tools, but a free version will always exist for everyday tracking.

Does it connect to my bank?

Not required. The app is designed to encourage intentional spending, so entering each spend yourself prompts greater awareness of your spending habits. It is quick, and it keeps you aware of every choice.

What devices does it work on?

The app is supported on most modern smartphones and computers running iOS, Android, Mac or Windows.

Can I use my own currency?

Yes. We support almost all major currencies.

When are you releasing the app?

We are releasing the beta version to small groups of users to give everyone the best experience. Please join the wait list and you will receive an email at the earliest!
