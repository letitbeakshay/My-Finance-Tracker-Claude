Questions

Good to know.

Is it free?

Yes, Faithful Portion is free to use right now. Later on we may add an optional paid plan with extra features, but we plan to always keep a free version for everyday tracking. If anything changes, people on the waiting list will hear about it first.

Does it connect to my bank?

No. You add each expense yourself, on purpose. It's quick, and it keeps you aware of every choice. You don't need to share any bank logins.

Which phones does it work on?

Any phone or computer with a modern browser: iPhone, Android, Mac or Windows. You can add it to your home screen and open it like an app.

Can I use my own currency?

Yes. Pick your currency in settings, such as dollars, pounds, euros, rupees, naira, pesos and more.

When can I start?

We're opening in small groups. Join the waiting list and we'll send you one email when your spot is ready.
